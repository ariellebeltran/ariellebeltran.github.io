Background

I have a BSc degree from the School of Interactive Arts & Technology with a concentration of Design (June 2025) from Simon Fraser University. My main skills are in user experience design, interface design, and web design. Other skills that I have experience in is graphic design, 3D-modeling, 3D printing and front-end developing.

I am currently trying to obtain a Google Digital Marketing and E-commerce certificate! I realized recently that user experience and digital marketing methodologies intertwine in relation to users or customers. I am always fascinated about the customer journey process and how, as a designer, I can improve their experiences.


Design Ethos

Design is a product of time.
At every phase of a design process, one thing is clear, we are the bridge that connects the user to a product. Good design is never static it grows, adapts, and evolves with time.
